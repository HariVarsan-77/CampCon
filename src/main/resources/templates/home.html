About Campus Connect

Empowering student voices and modernizing campus administration workflows.

Campus Connect was designed to replace paper-based complaint registers, informal email threads, and delayed verbal complaints with a transparent, responsive, and accountable digital pipeline.

Whether it is a broken projector interrupting lectures, hostel water heating failures, unsanitary dining conditions, or safety lighting issues on campus pathways—students can file detailed reports with photo evidence, receive official management replies, and track remediation progress step-by-step.

How It Works

A streamlined three-step workflow from problem identification to verified resolution.

1

1. Report

Students select an issue category, describe the problem, specify campus location, and attach photo evidence.

2

2. Review

College administrators and estate officers evaluate the submission, inspect priorities, and schedule campus staff.

3

3. Resolve

Management communicates official updates, rectifies the grievance, and marks the complaint verified and resolved.
